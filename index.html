Eligibility claims
Verify before creating or changing Eligibility_Rules. A submitted statement is evidence to review, not a verified rule.
Funding claims
Verify before creating or changing Program_Funding. Do not treat a provider submission alone as proof that a funding source is accepted.
Additional information

Deep Review
